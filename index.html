Our Specialized Services

Marine Support

Reliable Tug Boat support and specialized Barge solutions for transporting heavy equipment across inland and coastal waters.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Expert Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT), Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT), and Radiography to accurately detect flaws and surface discontinuities.

Industrial Inspection

Comprehensive Boiler inspections, Hydrostatic pressure testing up to 700 bar, and Ultrasonic Thickness Measurement (UTM) for corrosion monitoring.

Safety & Certification

Annual LSA & FFA certifications, life raft servicing, vacuum box leak tests, and wire rope sling load test validations.
