Dye Penetrant Test (DPT)

Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) is a non-destructive testing technique and also a cost-effective method used to locate surface breaking flaws such as cracks, porosity, laps, seams and other surface discontinuities.

Pressure Vessel Testing

At Echo Sound Inspection Services, we provide comprehensive pressure vessel testing to ensure safety, compliance, and optimal performance. Our certified inspectors use advanced techniques to detect flaws, assess integrity, and meet regulatory standards across various industries.

Vacuum Box Test

Vacuum box testing is a non-destructive examination used when trying to locate weld seam leaks. A vacuum box and a compressor create a high or low pressure vacuum while a detergent solution is applied to the test area. The detergent bubbles, making leaks visible within the created pressure envelope.

Chain Block Testing

Echo Sound Inspection Services provides dependable chain block testing to ensure that lifting equipment meets safety and operational standards. Our certified technicians conduct thorough load testing and inspections to confirm strength, functionality, and compliance with industry regulations.

Wire Rope Test

Echo Sound Inspection Services offers professional wire rope testing to ensure safety in lifting and rigging operations. Employing accurate inspection methods, we evaluate wear, corrosion, and load capacity to verify compliance with industry standards and maintain operational integrity.

Hydrostatic Test

ESIS used a compact portable air driven diaphragm style auto pump for the hydro test. Ideal for hydro testing heat exchanger tube, pipe and pressure vessels in the field with easy to read pressure gauges.
